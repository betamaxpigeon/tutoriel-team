ABOUT ME

My name is VHSCrow, and I'm 16 years old. My coding experience has come mostly from my father, who's a software engineer, and my own interests. I like indie games like Undertale and Deltarune by Toby Fox, Hollow Knight and it's sequel, Silksong by Team Cherry. I especially like rhythm games including osu!, A Dance of Fire and Ice, Geometry Dash, and others. I primarily do website based coding, but I've dabbled in Lua, C, C#, C++, GML, and Godot.
